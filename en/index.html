questions

Before you start

The most common questions about trak.

What do you get for free in trak?

The free plan tracks up to 2 habits and includes 2 wallpaper styles (Year calendar and Month by month), with no time limit. trak Pro unlocks unlimited habits, every style and the custom palette.

How much does trak Pro cost?

trak Pro comes as a monthly or an annual plan, with a 14-day free trial on the annual plan (7 days on the monthly one). Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store, and you can cancel any time in your account settings.

Does trak work on Android?

For now trak is iPhone only, running iOS 26 or later.

How does the calendar show up on the Lock Screen?

A shortcut in Apple's Shortcuts app updates your wallpaper automatically every night. There is a full step-by-step guide here.

Where is my data stored?

Your habits and preferences sync privately to your account. trak does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising — see the privacy policy.

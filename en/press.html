Press kit

Updated September 6, 2026 (version 1.1.0)

trak is the habit tracker that turns your consistency into your iPhone Lock Screen wallpaper — updated automatically, every morning, without opening the app. The thesis: the habit you see is the habit you keep.

Quick facts

  • What it is: habit tracker + automatic Lock Screen wallpaper (via iOS Shortcuts).
  • Launch: July 21, 2026, on the Brazilian and global App Store. Version 1.1.0 on September 6, 2026: your own photo as the wallpaper background, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the palette Collection and sharing as a story.
  • Platform: iPhone (iOS 26+), in Portuguese and English. 100% native: SwiftUI, SwiftData, App Intents, StoreKit 2.
  • Styles: 8 wallpapers (Year calendar, Month by month, Semester, Life in weeks, Consistency, Streak number, Pulse, Spiral), original palettes (Ember, Tide, Moss), the Collection (Lavender, Aurora, Amber, Charcoal), a cell-by-cell palette editor and, since 1.1.0, your own photo as the background with your progress on top.
  • Widgets (1.1.0): Home Screen (wallpaper thumbnail, a habit you can check off in one tap, today) and Lock Screen (circular and rectangular), in several styles.
  • Price: free (2 active habits, 2 styles); trak Pro at R$ 79.90/year (14-day free trial) or R$ 14.90/month (7-day free trial) — prices in BRL (Brazil).
  • Privacy: no ads, no tracking SDKs, no data selling. Passwordless sign-in (Apple/Google) and account deletion inside the app.
  • Who makes it: Tiago Villas, a solo developer in Brazil — from the design (Figma) to the backend.
  • App Store: apps.apple.com/br/app/id6771655767

The story in three lines

trak was born out of a personal frustration: every habit app ended up forgotten in a folder. The answer was not one more notification — it was putting your progress in the one place nobody can ignore: the Lock Screen. Built from scratch by a solo developer in Brazil, from the first sketch in Figma to launch.
